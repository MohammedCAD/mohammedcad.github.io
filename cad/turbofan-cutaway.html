Turbofan · internal assembly

Internal layout, coaxial assemblies and component clearances

Design & modelling approach

Cutaway interpretation

The sectional illustration exposes internal rotating and stationary components that are hidden in the exterior view. The central axis provides a principal datum for concentric compressor-like stages, casing segments and support elements.

Assembly definition

Internal parts are organised using common axial references and component mating geometry. A transparent or cut-away representation makes it possible to inspect stacked stage locations, shaft alignments and radial envelopes. Creo assembly placement and exploded or simplified representations are relevant for communicating this complexity.

CAD geometry

Revolved surfaces describe cylindrical and annular housings; patterned features distribute repeated fasteners and blades; swept or lofted profiles generate non-prismatic channels and transitions. Separate components are preferable to a single unified solid where motion, maintenance or assembly order matters.

Engineering interfaces

The section provides a basis for reviewing axial stack-up, radial clearance and accessible mounting interfaces. The rendering does not constitute proof of actual engine-stage performance or material qualification.
