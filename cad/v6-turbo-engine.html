V6 turbocharged engine

A multi-component Creo Parametric engine project organised around the engine structure, cranktrain, valve train, induction and exhaust systems.

Design & modelling approach

System integration

The native archive contains independent Creo components and subassemblies for a V6 engine concept: block and heads, piston/crank mechanism, cam and valve components, air-path and exhaust assemblies. The design is presented as a system of linked mechanical functions rather than a single CAD object.

Parametric design approach

The block is organised about cylinder-axis reference geometry and aligned mounting planes; the cranktrain relies on eccentric rotating journals and coaxial piston pins; the valve train introduces repeating driven components. Revolve, Extrude, Hole, Pattern and swept passages are relevant feature families for these mechanical elements.

Documentation and configuration

Creo’s revisioned .prt and .asm files make the project suitable for presenting part/assembly separation, interface-driven design and subsystem breakdown. Regeneration, constraint completeness and simulated operational performance are not evidenced by the supplied native inventory.
