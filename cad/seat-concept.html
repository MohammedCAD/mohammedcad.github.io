CAD CASE STUDY 09 / 12 · Product design / surface modelling

Seat concept

Contoured seating shell and integrated surfaces

Design & modelling approach

Product geometry

The seat concept combines a shaped backrest, cushion regions and side supports in an integrated profile. The surfaces are driven by human contact areas and their relation to a supporting structure.

Surface methodology

Splines and sectional curves control the outer silhouette; multi-section and boundary-blend surfaces form the continuous seat shell; Trim or Split define openings and different insert regions. Offset and Thick Surface are possible steps toward a manufacturable shell.

Design development

The important next checks concern ergonomics, support stiffness, attachment loads, foam allowances, seatbelt interfaces if applicable and production tolerances. The model is a form study, not a certified occupant-restraint design.
