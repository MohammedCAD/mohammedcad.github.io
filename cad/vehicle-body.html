Vehicle body concept

Exterior body form and wheel enclosure

Design & modelling approach

Exterior design

The image shows a low-profile vehicle concept with a continuous hood-to-cabin transition, wheel envelopes and an open front or rear body region. Major surfaces need coherent proportions and consistent transitions across the vehicle.

Surface modelling workflow

A controlled reference skeleton fixes wheelbase, symmetry plane and major design curves. Multi-Sections Surface or Boundary Blend creates hood and fender regions; Symmetry/Mirror keeps opposite sides consistent; Split defines openings and interfaces.

Practical evaluation

Surface curvature continuity matters for highlight flow and perceived quality. Packaging around wheels, suspension travel, doors and occupant spaces should be checked before converting a concept exterior into a production body.
