CAD CASE STUDY 05 / 12 · Mechanical assembly / CAD

Compact engine concept

Engine assembly geometry and component positioning

Design & modelling approach

Visible architecture

The drawing presents an engine concept with opposed cylinder-bank geometry, a central housing and prominent end features. Differentiating crankcase, bank structures, manifolds and attached components allows a readable assembly hierarchy.

Parametric geometry

Extrude generates prismatic structures; Revolve defines hubs, pulleys and rotational journals; Hole and Pattern create repeated fastening and cylinder-interface geometry. Assembly datums fix angular bank orientation and the centreline relationship between supporting parts.

Assembly considerations

The relationship between the engine block, cylinder banks, front accessories and fluid-path components determines packaging volume, maintenance accessibility and interference constraints. A CAD visualisation should not be treated as a torque or power validation.
