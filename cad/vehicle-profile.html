Vehicle exterior · profile

Surface proportions, wheel arches and side profile

Design & modelling approach

Profile intent

The side view isolates the most important proportions: wheelbase, wheel diameter, roof height, front/rear overhangs and greenhouse transition. It provides a useful design reference for subsequent 3D surface development.

Geometric controls

Splines and constraint-driven sketches can capture the silhouette. A centre plane, station sections and mirrored geometry help retain symmetry; lofted surfaces then carry the profile into the three-dimensional body.

Design assessment

A profile drawing is a good starting point for packaging review, but aerodynamic drag, crashworthiness and manufacturability require additional analyses and design evidence.
