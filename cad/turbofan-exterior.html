CAD CASE STUDY 01 / 12 · Propulsion system / CAD

Turbofan · exterior

Circular inlet, nacelle envelope and radial fan geometry

Design & modelling approach

System overview

The front view shows the nacelle envelope, inlet lip and radial fan visible inside the circular inlet. The design relies on a common engine centreline, coaxial circular profiles and an orderly repetition of blade geometry.

Component modelling

Revolved profiles are natural for the nacelle inlet and rotational housings. The exterior cover can be established from longitudinal profiles and lofted transition sections, with rounds used to control changes around the intake lip. Fan blades are shaped from aerodynamic surfaces around a hub and distributed about the engine axis using a circular component pattern.

Creo assembly structure

The submitted jetmotorcomplete.asm.1 establishes that this project includes a native Creo assembly rather than only a rendered image. The assembly structure is the correct level for defining positioning references among the nacelle, fan, shaft and surrounding engine components. Separate part histories would be needed to reconstruct the actual modelling sequence.

Design review

Relevant checks include radial tip clearance, concentricity, interference between blade tips and casing, access for assembly and consistency of the inlet flow-path shape. Aerodynamic efficiency, thermal behaviour and structural safety cannot be inferred from CAD appearance.
