CAD CASE STUDY 07 / 12 · Parametric solid modelling

Engine housing · second view

Second drawing of the housing with cylindrical bores

Design & modelling approach

Secondary view

The alternate camera position clarifies the relationships between cylindrical recesses, outer housing walls and the upper geometry. The feature arrangement must remain controlled by the same reference dimensions as the first view.

Feature strategy

A shared datum-based hole pattern preserves spacing and alignment across design changes; shaped extrusions and local pockets form surrounding mounting geometry. Large rounds can be applied after the primary volume and cutout topology are stable.

Detail checks

This view is helpful for spotting thin ligaments, inaccessible internal corners and transitions that might complicate tooling or stress evaluation. Quantitative clearance or strength claims require the solid model and design conditions.
