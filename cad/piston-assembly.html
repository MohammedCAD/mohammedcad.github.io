Piston and crank mechanism

Kinematic relationships, cylinder axes and rotating parts

Design & modelling approach

Mechanism architecture

The assembly illustration presents piston-like bodies linked toward a central crankshaft. The geometry demonstrates the conversion between reciprocating motion along cylinder axes and rotation about the crank axis.

Part-level geometry

Revolve suits axisymmetric piston crowns, skirts, pins and journals. Extrude and Hole establish connecting-rod webs, split caps and pin bores. The offset between crank axis and crankpin defines throw; the full stroke in an ideal slider-crank mechanism is twice the throw.

Assembly and kinematics

Coaxial pin connections and sliding cylinder guidance permit the intended movement without overconstraining it. To check a full revolution, the assembly needs defined joints, realistic clearances and contact or interference analysis, which are not evidenced by the static image.
