CAD CASE STUDY 06 / 12 · Parametric solid modelling

Engine housing · geometry

Repeated bores, datum references and machined interfaces

Design & modelling approach

Functional geometry

The housing shows multiple adjacent cylindrical openings within a common cast-like body. The bores set the core spacing constraints; surrounding planar and curved regions form the body envelope and potential mounting interfaces.

Parametric construction

A master sketch or datum layout fixes bore centre positions. Extrude creates the bulk housing and end flanges; repeated Hole or Extruded Cut operations establish the cylindrical voids. Rounds and blends reduce abrupt geometric transitions while leaving adequate material between neighbouring openings.

Engineering review

Wall thickness between bores, perpendicularity to mating faces, available fastening land and fixture accessibility must be considered before manufacture. Dimensions and tolerances are not provided in the rendering.
